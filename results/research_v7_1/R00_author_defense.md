# R00: what the authors explain, and what remains to test

The main contribution is a computationally motivated single-level VI with differentiable follower responses. Non-MaaS multimodal access, MoD service coverage, transfer convenience, asymmetric Sioux wholesale prices, and future demand/contract/platform extensions are explicitly discussed. These are not missing definitions.

The appropriate review asks how those definitions map to an external market and how counterfactual outcomes respond to changed conditions. No empirical calibration is established by merely reading a conceptual justification. OD VOT sensitivity and the 150 local initialization experiments deserve explicit credit.

Two additional source-to-code qualifications matter: page 10 prescribes total-demand homogeneous MoD access, while our November baseline uses node aggregation; page 10 and page 19 describe different residual functions for EG. We retain the independent baseline without quietly claiming either discrepancy is an author error. Page 7 also explicitly qualifies VI stationarity versus nonconvex global profit maximization.

For each of the 21 issues, paper_defense_ledger.csv records pages, section, evidence class, author explanation, remaining test and a presentation-safe claim. No claim of Lausanne calibration is made.
